About Adkins Benefits

Insurance Guidance Without the Pressure

Since 2010, Adkins Benefits has helped individuals and families understand their insurance options and make decisions with more confidence. Our role is not to push you toward a particular policy. We start by learning about your situation, the coverage you already have, and what you actually need.

We help with Medicare, individual and family health insurance, life insurance, and mortgage protection. Depending on the situation, that may mean comparing new coverage, reviewing an existing policy, or simply confirming that what you already have is still a good fit.

There is never a charge for our services. If you decide to purchase coverage through us, we are compensated by the insurance company rather than charging you a consulting or broker fee.

How We Work

A Simpler Way to Review Insurance

We Start With Your Situation

We review your needs, budget, current coverage, and priorities before discussing possible changes.

We Compare the Options

When appropriate, we compare available plans and companies so you can understand the differences rather than trying to sort through them alone.

We Explain the Tradeoffs

Price matters, but it is not the only consideration. We help you understand benefits, limitations, and how the coverage fits your goals.

We Don't Recommend Change Just to Make a Sale

Sometimes the best result of a review is discovering that your current coverage is still the right choice.

Let's Review Your Coverage

Whether you're comparing new coverage or wondering if what you already have is still right for you, we're here to help make the process easier.
